# Meridian Shipping - Technical Procurement Policy (TP-02, rev. 5)

## Approval thresholds
Purchase orders for spares are approved according to total order value including freight:
- Up to 5,000 USD: the vessel's Chief Engineer may approve.
- 5,000 to 25,000 USD: the Technical Superintendent must approve.
- Above 25,000 USD: the Fleet Technical Manager must approve, and at least two supplier quotations are required.

## Supplier selection
Preferred suppliers are Nordic Marine Parts (European engines and separators) and Pacific Ship Supply (Asian ports and Wartsila equipment). Where a part is available from both preferred suppliers, choose the lower total landed cost unless the lead time difference exceeds 14 days.
Parts that require a class certificate must be ordered from a supplier that can issue one.

## Critical spares
Critical spares (cylinder liners, turbocharger cartridges, exhaust valve spindles and main bearing shells) must be kept on board at a minimum of 1 unit per vessel. When stock of a critical spare falls to zero, a replacement order must be raised within 24 hours.

## Urgent orders
Urgent or express shipments require a written justification from the Chief Engineer stating the risk to vessel operation. Surcharges for urgent shipments are charged to the vessel's operating budget.
