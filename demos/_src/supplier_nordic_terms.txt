NORDIC MARINE PARTS AS - SUPPLY TERMS FOR MERIDIAN SHIPPING
Framework agreement NMP-2026-017, valid 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2027.

1. PRICING AND DISCOUNTS
Prices in the Meridian spares catalogue are net prices in USD, excluding freight. A volume discount of 5% applies to any single purchase order above 10,000 USD, and 8% above 25,000 USD. Discounts are not cumulative.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
Payment is due 45 days from invoice date. Late payments incur interest of 1% per month. Orders above 50,000 USD require a 30% advance payment.

3. DELIVERY
Lead times in the catalogue are counted in calendar days from order confirmation to dispatch from Bergen, Norway. Delivery is DAP (Delivered at Place) to the named port agent. Air freight to any port in Europe is charged at a flat 350 USD per shipment; outside Europe at 4% of order value with a minimum of 600 USD.
Urgent orders (AOG - vessel at risk of off-hire) can be dispatched within 72 hours for stock items, with a surcharge of 15%.

4. WARRANTY AND RETURNS
All parts carry a warranty of 12 months from installation or 18 months from delivery, whichever comes first. Unused parts in original packaging may be returned within 60 days for a 20% restocking fee. Cylinder liners and turbocharger parts are non-returnable.

5. QUALITY AND CERTIFICATION
All parts are genuine OEM or OEM-approved and are supplied with a certificate of conformity. Class certificates (DNV, ABS, LR) can be provided on request at 120 USD per certificate.
