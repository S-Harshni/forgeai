VESSEL CONNECTIVITY FAQ
Each vessel has a VSAT link with a monthly data allowance of 200 GB for business use. Crew welfare internet is a separate 100 GB allowance.
When the business allowance is exceeded, speed is reduced to 2 Mbps until the end of the month. The master may request a one-off top-up of 50 GB, which costs 400 USD and is charged to the vessel budget.
Software updates for navigation systems (ECDIS) must only be installed by the vendor or under remote supervision of the Marine IT team, never by crew alone.
Shore staff must not send attachments larger than 10 MB to vessels; use the file transfer portal instead.
