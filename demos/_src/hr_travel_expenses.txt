MERIDIAN SHIPPING LTD - TRAVEL AND EXPENSES POLICY
Policy FIN-11, version 2.0, effective 1 March 2026. Applies to all shore staff travelling on company business, including vessel visits and port inspections.

1. BOOKING TRAVEL
All business travel must be booked through the corporate travel desk at least 14 days before departure where possible. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class requires written approval from a department director.
Rail travel should be standard class. Taxis are permitted when public transport is unavailable or between 22:00 and 06:00.

2. ACCOMMODATION
Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 180 USD per night in standard locations and up to 260 USD per night in high-cost cities such as Singapore, London, Oslo and Tokyo. Staff visiting a vessel in port may stay on board where the master agrees, in which case no hotel is booked.

3. MEALS AND DAILY ALLOWANCE
A daily meal allowance of 65 USD is paid for each full day of travel, or 35 USD for a partial day. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment must be pre-approved by a line manager and is limited to 90 USD per person.

4. CLAIMING EXPENSES
Expense claims must be submitted in the expense system within 30 days of returning from the trip, with itemised receipts for every item above 25 USD. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.
Corporate credit cards are issued to employees who travel more than 4 times per year. Personal use of a corporate card is a disciplinary offence.

5. MILEAGE
Use of a private car for business is reimbursed at 0.45 USD per kilometre. Commuting between home and the normal office is not reimbursable.
