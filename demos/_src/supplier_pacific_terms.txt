PACIFIC SHIP SUPPLY PTE LTD - CONDITIONS OF SALE (MERIDIAN SHIPPING)
Agreement PSS/MS/2026, valid from 1 April 2026 for 24 months.

1. PRICES
Catalogue prices are in USD, ex-works Singapore, excluding freight and local taxes. Orders below 500 USD carry a small-order handling fee of 45 USD. No volume discounts are offered; instead, prices are fixed for the duration of the agreement.

2. PAYMENT
Payment terms are 30 days from invoice date. A 2% early-payment discount applies if payment is received within 10 days.

3. DELIVERY AND LEAD TIMES
Catalogue lead times are calendar days from order to dispatch from Singapore. Delivery to vessels in Singapore, Port Klang and Jakarta is free of charge by launch boat. Other ports: courier freight is charged at cost plus 10%.
Express dispatch within 48 hours is available for in-stock items with a surcharge of 10%, minimum 150 USD.

4. WARRANTY AND RETURNS
Warranty is 12 months from delivery. Returns are accepted within 30 days, unused and in original packaging, with a 15% restocking fee. Electrical parts and gaskets are non-returnable.

5. CERTIFICATES
Parts are supplied with a manufacturer certificate. Class society certificates are not available from Pacific Ship Supply; where class certification is mandatory, order from an alternative supplier.
