MERIDIAN SHIPPING LTD - SHORE STAFF LEAVE POLICY
Policy HR-04, version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all shore-based employees. Seafarers are covered by the separate Crew Employment Agreement.

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time shore staff receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees with more than 5 years of continuous service receive 27 working days. Part-time employees receive leave pro-rata to their contracted hours.
Leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences longer than 3 days. Requests are approved by the line manager.
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire. Unused leave beyond the carry-over limit is not paid out, except on termination of employment.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any absence of more than 2 consecutive working days. Employees must notify their line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
Long-term illness beyond 12 days is handled under the Income Protection scheme, which pays 75% of base salary for up to 26 weeks.

3. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers receive 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Employees must have completed 6 months of service to be eligible for paid parental leave.
Notice of intended parental leave must be given to HR at least 8 weeks before the expected start date.

4. COMPASSIONATE AND OTHER LEAVE
Compassionate leave of up to 5 paid days is granted on the death of an immediate family member, and up to 2 paid days for an extended family member.
Employees called for jury service receive full pay for the duration of the service. Employees may take 1 paid volunteering day per year with a registered charity.

5. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
Shore staff observe the public holidays of the country of their office. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a replacement day off within 30 days.
