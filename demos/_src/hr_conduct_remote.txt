MERIDIAN SHIPPING LTD - HYBRID WORK AND CODE OF CONDUCT
Policy HR-07, version 1.4, effective 1 June 2026. Applies to all shore staff.

1. HYBRID WORKING
Shore staff may work remotely for up to 2 days per week, subject to line manager agreement. Operations centre staff on duty rotas must work on site during their rostered shifts. Remote work from another country requires HR approval and is limited to 20 working days per year.
The company provides a one-time home office allowance of 300 USD for equipment, claimable within the first 3 months of employment or of starting a hybrid arrangement.

2. WORKING HOURS
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Standard working time is 37.5 hours per week. Overtime for eligible grades must be pre-approved and is compensated as time off in lieu at a rate of 1.5 hours per hour worked.

3. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees must not accept gifts or hospitality worth more than 50 USD from suppliers, agents or charterers. Any gift above this value must be declared to the Compliance team within 5 working days.
Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with a line manager, with HR, or anonymously through the Speak Up hotline, which is operated by an independent provider and available 24 hours a day.

4. INFORMATION SECURITY
Company laptops must be locked when unattended. Phishing emails must be reported using the Report Phish button. Vessel position data and cargo manifests are confidential and must not be shared outside the company.

5. DISCIPLINARY PROCESS
Breaches of this policy are handled under the disciplinary procedure. Employees have the right to be accompanied by a colleague at any formal disciplinary meeting and may appeal a decision within 10 working days.
